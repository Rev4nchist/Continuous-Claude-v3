Choose the work surface, not just the skin.

These prototypes test four different answers to one question: where should people meet the Agentic OS? Explore each, then score the product assumptions that matter to you.

Prototype boundary

All data and actions are simulated. Controls demonstrate governed commands, evidence, approvals, and recovery. They do not invoke agents or modify Notion.

Notion = human intentProjects, tasks, curated knowledge and approval inbox.
Git = executable definitionsVersioned workflows, skills, policies, schemas and evals.
PostgreSQL = live runtimeRuns, attempts, events, leases, operational approvals and audit.
