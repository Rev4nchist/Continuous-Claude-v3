## Description

Van Neistat has 26 years of footage. 9 terabytes, 451 hours, 48,000 clips, and no way to find anything in it. We built a crawler that pulls a few frames from every clip, has a free local vision model describe them, and writes it all to a text file. 9 terabytes of video becomes 12 megabytes of words. The computer searches the words.

No cloud. No API bill. It ran on a Mac Mini for two days.
